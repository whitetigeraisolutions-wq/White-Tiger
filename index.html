What are automations? +
Automations are pre-programmed actions that run on their own — at the right time, on the right trigger, with no one clicking anything. Zero clicks. The work just happens.
How can automations help my business? +
Automations increase efficiency. Once set up, they work without human interference — 24/7. Tasks that needed five people can now be handled by just one. Your team stops doing busywork and starts doing the work that actually grows the business.
What kind of computer do I need? +
An Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) with 16 GB of memory minimum. Currently we set up using Claude, which only ships a Mac app — so a compatible Mac is required. We'll confirm your specific machine works during the scoping call before any commitment.
Where do you operate? +
We currently service Ahmedabad, Vadodara and Surat. All setups are done in person, on your location. We bring the build to you.
What if my workflow isn't on your list? +
The list above is just a starting point. We build fully custom zero-click automations for whatever your business actually does — even if it's nothing on this page. Tell us the workflow, we'll tell you how we'd automate it.

It depends on how many automations you want and whether any need third-party plugins. We give you a full itemised quote before any work starts — no surprise invoices. The starting point is around ₹30,000 all-in for setup plus your first automation.
How long does it take? +
A typical stack takes 1–2 weeks from kickoff to live. Bigger or more complex builds take longer — we'll tell you the timeline upfront.
